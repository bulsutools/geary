Graduating with Latin Honors at BulSU comes down to your overall grade point average (GPA), clean grades in every subject, residency at the university, and maintaining at least 15 units per term (governed alongside **BOR Resolution No. 47, S. 2018**, confirmed active in Student Government Academic Affairs advisories). BulSU also awards non-Latin graduation honors and the annual **Gold Gear Awards** for top semester performers.

## Latin Honors GPA Cutoffs

| Overall GPA | Degree Courses | Non-Degree Courses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1.00 - 1.20** | Summa Cum Laude | With Highest Honors |
| **1.21 - 1.45** | Magna Cum Laude | With High Honors |
| **1.46 - 1.75** | Cum Laude | With Honors |

## Non-Negotiable Latin Honors Rules

Meeting the GPA bracket is only part of the check. Under Section Q of the Student Manual, every candidate must satisfy all three rules:

1. **No grade lower than `2.0`** (or equivalent) in **any subject** ever enrolled.
2. **Minimum BulSU residency and credits:** Completed at least **75% of total academic units** required for the course and stayed at least **two (2) years in residence** at BulSU.
3. **Minimum 15 units every term:** Enrolled at least **15 credit units** prescribed in the curriculum in every term or semester. Exceptions apply only if:
   - Your curriculum itself schedules fewer than 15 units in that semester, or
   - Needed subjects are not offered that term.

## Other Graduation Awards

Even if one mark keeps you out of Latin Honors, BulSU recognizes top graduates in four other categories:

- **Outstanding Student Award (1 per program per college/campus):** GPA of at least `1.75`, no grade below `2.0`, minimum 15 units, plus wins in co-curricular/extra-curricular contests and active membership in recognized university or college orgs.
- **Best in Specialization Award (1 per specialization):** Top major performance with no grade below `2.0` in all major subjects and competition wins, chosen by a faculty specialization committee.
- **College Leadership Award (1 per college/campus):** Enlisted at least 15 units for the last two semesters with zero dropped, `Inc.`, or failing grades, advisor-nominated, strong leadership record, and zero disciplinary violations.
- **University Leadership Award (1 university-wide):** Open to graduating leaders from Student Government, recognized orgs, cultural groups, or student publications upon submitting a leadership folio to the Dean of SAS.

## Annual Gold Gear Awards (President's & Dean's Lists)

While you are still studying (2nd year through senior year), the Office of Student Affairs and Services holds the annual **Gold Gear Awards** based on your **last two consecutive semesters** (2nd semester of the previous academic year and 1st semester of the current academic year):

- **President's List:** GPA of **1.20 to 1.00**, with no grade lower than **2.0** in any subject.
- **Dean's List:** GPA of **1.75 to 1.21**, with no grade lower than **2.0** in any subject.

Both awards require at least **15 academic units** (or the prescribed regular curriculum load) across those two terms and **zero disciplinary record** for the current academic year. Individual colleges announce their own Gold Gear filing deadlines and instructions (for example, submitting a scanned Certificate of Grades in a brown envelope and filling out a college Google Form to the Dean's Office), so watch your college's official page around December and January.
