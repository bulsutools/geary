Incoming applicants must pass the **Bulacan State University Admission Test (BSUAT)**, which you may take **only once**, alongside meeting program GPA thresholds and document checks run by the Office of Admissions and Orientation.

## Student classifications

| Classification | Definition and Key Limits |
| :--- | :--- |
| Regular student | Carries the full prescribed unit load per semester in a curriculum leading to a degree or certificate. |
| Irregular student | Carries fewer than the full prescribed load due to failing marks, shifting, or cross-enrollment. |
| Shifter | Moves from one course or program to another within BulSU. |
| Transfer student | Entered from another recognized college or university with valid transfer credentials. |
| Working student | Employed while studying; must carry at least `15` academic units per semester. |
| Foreign student | Requires a student visa cleared by the Bureau of Immigration and pays prescribed foreign tuition and miscellaneous rates. |
| Special student | Non-credit listener limited to `9` units per semester for at most `2` academic years (unless VPAA approves an exception), graded only as satisfactory or unsatisfactory. |

## Who can take the BSUAT

Applicants qualify for admission testing if they fall into any of these groups:

- Grade 12 graduates or Senior High School graduates (including recognized foreign K-12 graduates or candidates).
- High school graduates from the pre-K-12 secondary curriculum who have not enrolled in any college program.
- **PEPT** qualifiers with an average of at least **`82%`** across Math, Communication Arts (English), Science, Communication Arts (Filipino), and Araling Panlipunan.
- **ALS A&E Secondary** passers with a Standard Score of at least **`100`** and an essay proficiency rating of **`2` or higher**.

Under `RA 7277`, qualified persons with disabilities (`PWDs`) are eligible for admission on equal terms without discrimination.

## Application and BSUAT steps

1. Bring original documents plus **one photocopy** to the Office of Admissions and Orientation (`Form 138` with at least second-grading marks for incoming freshmen; `COR` and class cards for shifters, transferees, and third-year `BIT` applicants; or `TOR` for second-course and `CPTE` applicants).
2. Prepare two `2"x2"` colored ID pictures on a white background (computer-generated prints are not accepted), a copy of your school ID, and a completed application form. Authorized representatives such as parents, relatives, teachers, or guardians may file on your behalf.
3. Pay the **`P300.00` non-refundable** examination fee at the Cashier's Office.
4. Present the Official Receipt (`OR`) to the Office of Admissions and Orientation to receive your exam permit and schedule.

## Shortlisted post-BSUAT requirements

If you pass the BSUAT and qualify for your chosen program (applicants who miss their first-choice GPA cutoff may still be placed in their second-choice program if slots remain), submit these originals upon enrollment:

1. Reservation slip.
2. Original **Form 138** (Report Card) and Senior High School diploma where applicable.
3. **NSO Birth Certificate** (submit a notarized *Affidavit of Discrepancy* if your birth certificate name differs from your school records).
4. **Certificate of Good Moral Character** signed by the School Principal or Guidance Counselor with dry seal.
5. Medical permit from the **BulSU Clinic**.
6. One recent `2"x2"` colored photo with a white background and completed college interview (when required by the college).

Falsified or tampered documents lead to immediate disqualification.
