Part I and Part III of the Student Manual set out concrete academic protections from the Education Act of 1982 alongside formal grievance steps handled through the **Director for Student Welfare (`DSW`)** and Student Government.

## Academic, record, and due process protections

Every bona fide BulSU undergraduate holds these enforceable rights:

- Inspect class records, verify how grades are calculated, and receive official certificates, diplomas, `TOR`, and transfer credentials within **30 days from filing**.
- Keep academic and personal records confidential unless authorized by the student or law.
- Publish student newspapers, form or join recognized campus organizations, and stay free from involuntary contributions unless approved by your own organization.
- In any disciplinary complaint, benefit from prior due process, require substantial evidence from the complainant, inspect and rebut all evidence, defend yourself personally or through counsel, and keep active student status until a decision issues (unless preventive suspension applies).

## How to file a student grievance

**Only the alleged victim** may initiate a formal grievance; third-party proxies cannot file on another student's behalf.

### Step 1: Direct resolution (optional)

Before elevating a complaint formally, students are encouraged (though not forced) to raise the concern directly or in writing with the faculty member, employee, or person involved, who is expected to respond promptly.

### Step 2: Formal complaint through DSW

If direct dialogue feels unsafe or fails to fix the issue:

1. File a formal grievance through the **Student Government** or directly with the **Director for Student Welfare (`DSW`)**. If the grievance is directed against the `DSW`, bring it straight to the **Dean of OSAS**.
2. The `DSW` checks the validity of the claim, mediates between parties or endorses the complaint to the office with authority, and monitors follow-up action.
3. If the responsible office fails to act, `DSW` escalates both the original complaint and an added report on official inaction up through supervisory levels, reaching the **University President** if unresolved.
