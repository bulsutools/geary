Campus canteens, the University Hostel, accredited outside dormitories, and BulSU varsity programs each carry specific conduct rules, privacy boundaries, and eligibility standards under Part III of the Student Manual.

## Canteen and food service rules

Canteen concessionaires undergo annual `MOH`/`DOH` health checks and random microbiological food testing. Students using campus dining areas must follow self-service lines, bus soiled trays and empty bottles to designated tray stations and bins, keep noise low, and avoid lingering at tables during peak breakfast and lunch hours.

## Dormitory accreditation and hostel room privacy

External boarding houses seeking accreditation are inspected by the **Housing Committee** (chaired by `DSW` with Safety and Security, Housing, `SG` President, and `PTA` President). Athlete quarters fall under the Sports Development Head, and BulSU is not liable for lost, damaged, or stolen personal items in dormitories.

Inside the **University Hostel**, student residents have privacy rights. University staff may enter a student's room **only** under these eight conditions:

1. An immediate emergency or safety threat.
2. Express permission from the room resident.
3. Execution of a valid search warrant.
4. Official legal duty of authorized university employees.
5. Loud audio or noise violating quiet study and sleeping hours.
6. Scheduled maintenance, repairs, or safety inspection.
7. Room closing or student check-out.
8. Official between-semester or Christmas break periods.

## Varsity sports tryouts and recruitment

Students can join BulSU varsity squads through campus tryouts, intramural scouting, or outside recruitment. External recruits must hold at least a **Palarong Pambansa bronze**, **Regional Meet silver**, or **Provincial Meet gold** medal (Division or District level finishes do not qualify).

Candidates are scored on five weights: **`30%` Attitude**, **`20%` Competition Level**, **`20%` Sports Skills**, **`20%` Grades**, and **`10%` Age**. Varsity athletes competing in `SCUAA` and `UCLAA` qualify for `50% to 100%` tuition scholarships provided failing grades remain at or below `10%` of enrolled units, and all off-campus games require Sports Head approval, medical clearance, and parental consent.
