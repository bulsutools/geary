In BulSU's student manual, a shifter is any enrolled student moving from one course to another, whether inside the same college or across another college or campus. Your exact steps depend on whether you are changing degree programs, switching majors, or transferring from a satellite campus. **Never apply through the Freshman Admission portal as a shifter:** Under BulSU's Freshman Undergraduate Admissions Policy, shifters who attempt to apply through Freshman Admission will be disallowed from all BulSU programs.

## When You Must Take the Shifter's Exam

Not every internal move needs a new entrance test:

- **Shifting between degree programs** (for example, Education to Engineering, or Engineering to Architecture): You must take the Shifter's Examination.
- **Changing majors within the same program:** You are exempted from taking the Shifter's Exam.
- **Shifting from an external campus:** Students coming from any BulSU satellite campus (Bustos, San Jose Del Monte, Matungao, or Hagonoy) must take the Shifter's Examination.
- **Continuing from a ladderized program:** If you finished a ladderized track and want to continue toward a bachelor's degree, you must take the continuing examination.

## How to Apply as a Shifter

In recent cycles (such as the May 2026 **BulSU Admissions Office** advisories), internal shifters apply alongside transferees and second-degree coursers during a short late-May online window followed by scheduled hard-copy appointments at Malolos Campus. To qualify voluntarily as a shifter, you need satisfactory grades. Watch **BulSU Admissions Office** announcements for open program slots across Main Campus and external campuses (**Bustos, Hagonoy, Meneses, San Rafael, and Sarmiento**), apply at `https://admissions.bulsu.edu.ph/` (or the Admissions Office), and prepare:

- Original and one photocopy of your Certificate of Registration (COR) and class cards
- Receipt for the required examination fee at the Cashier's Office if your move requires testing
- Annual physical, medical, and dental examination through the BulSU Medical Clinic (mandatory for shifters alongside freshmen and transferees)

## Shifting Due to Academic Delinquency

BulSU also allows or recommends shifting when grades slip under the Academic Delinquency rules:

- **Failed or dropped two subjects while on probation:** You are dropped from your current course, though you may apply to shift to another course with acceptance from the receiving Dean and approval from the Vice President for Academic Affairs (VPAA).
- **Failed 50% to 75% of your enrolled subjects:** The university recommends shifting to another program, subject to the receiving Dean's acceptance and VPAA approval.

If you fail more than 75% of your enrolled subjects in one semester (or 50% or more while on academic probation), you face disqualification from the university entirely. Check [What Happens When You Fail a Subject?](/articles/what-happens-when-you-fail-a-subject) before grades reach that threshold.

Remember too that BulSU tracks a maximum residency period from your initial entry (6 years for a 4-year course, 7.5 years for a 5-year course), so shifting late can affect your remaining subsidized terms. Read [Academic Load, Prerequisites, and Residency](/articles/academic-load-prerequisites-and-residency) to plan your remaining semesters.
