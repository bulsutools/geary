The **Office of Scholarship and Financial Assistance (OSFA)** manages university tuition waivers, external government and private grants, and paid part-time **Student Assistantships (`S.A.`)**. Failing grades (`5.00`) or unresolved `Inc.` marks put scholarship renewal at risk because OSFA evaluates academic records every semester before endorsement.

## Institutional scholarships and tuition discounts

Institutional scholarships cover either **100% (Full)** or **50% (Partial)** of tuition fees per semester.

| Scholarship Type | Discount | Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| High School Valedictorian | `100%` | Incoming freshman who graduated top of a recognized high school class of at least **100 students** (Principal-certified). |
| High School Salutatorian | `50%` | Incoming freshman who graduated second in a recognized high school class of at least **100 students** (Principal-certified). |
| Full Institutional Scholar | `100%` | GWA of `1.50` or better, no grade lower than `2.0`, enrolled in at least `15` units over the past two semesters (also awarded to the Student Government President). |
| Partial Institutional Scholar | `50%` | GWA of `1.75` or better, no grade lower than `2.0`, enrolled in at least `15` units over the past two semesters (also open to the Student Government Vice President). |
| BulSU Employee Dependent | `100%` | No failing mark in any subject; requires latest COG, parent's BulSU ID, and birth certificate. |
| Cultural Talents | `50% to 100%` | Qualified members of Hyper-Dynamics, Band, *Lahing Kayumanggi*, *Saring Himig*, or *Euphonistas* endorsed by their adviser. |
| Varsity Athletes | `50% to 100%` | Recommended by the coach based on competition tier (`SCUAA`, `UCLAA`, etc.), with failing marks kept at or below `10%` of enrolled units. |

## Barangay, government, and private grants

OSFA also endorses students for external assistance programs when slots open:

- **Barangay Scholarship (`RA 7160`):** Covers `100%` of tuition for children of elective Barangay officials upon submitting a DILG certification of the parent's post and term alongside the latest COG. Children of Barangay Tanods do not qualify.
- **National agencies:** Includes `CHED` merit and grant-in-aid programs (such as Full Merit, Partial Merit, SAFE, Grant-in-Aid, and `OTOS`), `DOST-SEI`, `GSIS`, `OWWA` (dependents of OFWs earning `US$400` or below), `PVAO`, `AFP`, Congressional grants, and `ESGP-PA` for `DSWD 4Ps` households.
- **LGU and private benefactors:** Unless a sponsor enforces stricter rules, applicants generally need a GPA of `2.0` or better in at least `15` enrolled units with no grade of `3.0` or lower in the prior semester.

### Common screening packet for endorsed grants

1. Proof of official admission to BulSU and complete admission papers.
2. Latest Certification of Grades (`COG`) or Form 138 (ideally `80%` rating or better).
3. Barangay Certificate of Indigency and proof of household billing.
4. Certificate of Good Moral Character.
5. Objective written assessment, essay, and validation interview with OSFA.

## Student Assistantship (S.A.) program

Undergraduates who want paid part-time office assignments on campus apply directly with an application letter addressed to OSFA. Applicants must carry **no failing (`5.00`) or incomplete (`Inc.`) grades** from the previous semester, pass the requesting office's pre-hiring screening, and turn in signed **parental consent**.
