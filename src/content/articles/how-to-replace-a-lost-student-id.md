BulSU enforces a strict **No ID, No Entry** policy at every campus gate. If you lose or damage your university ID, apply for a duplicate right away at the Office of the University Registrar. A duplicate ID costs **PHP 150** and takes about **2 working days** to print and release.

Tracking what you have gathered? [Check off your requirements in the Document Request Wizard](/wizard#student-id-replacement).

## Documents to prepare

You must be a currently enrolled student to request a duplicate ID. Gather these mandatory requirements before lining up at the Registrar:

- 1 original valid ID (National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or another accepted ID)
- Original notarized Affidavit of Loss from a notary public
- Official Receipt for PHP 150 from the Cashier's Office

If someone else is filing or claiming for you, also prepare a signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney, plus 1 photocopy of your ID and 1 photocopy of your representative's ID.

## Apply for a duplicate student ID

1. Notify both the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) and the Office of the University Registrar right away so your lost ID is logged.
2. Have a notary public prepare and notarize an Affidavit of Loss stating how and when your student ID went missing.
3. Present your valid ID at the Registrar's window so staff can verify your active enrollment in the student portal and issue your payment slip.
4. Pay **PHP 150** at the Cashier's Office using the payment slip and keep the Official Receipt.
5. Hand your Affidavit of Loss, Official Receipt, and required IDs to the designated Registrar window to receive your claim stub while staff verify your record and print the duplicate card.
6. Return on the scheduled release date, usually after **2 working days**, with your claim stub and valid ID to claim your duplicate ID.

## Enter campus while waiting for your ID

You have 2 temporary options at the campus gate, depending on whether your ID is lost or left at home:

| Situation | Gate entry rule |
| --- | --- |
| Lost ID | Security accepts your notarized Affidavit of Loss as a temporary gate pass for **3 days only**. File your duplicate ID request right away so you are not locked out after day 3. |
| ID left at home | Present your current Certificate of Registration (COR) to the gate guard, who will write a remark, date it, and sign over their printed name on the back before letting you in. |

## Cancel a loss report if you find your ID

If you find your original ID after reporting it missing, visit OSAS to cancel the loss report on your file.

## Penalties for ID tampering

Never alter details on a student ID, swap photos, or lend your card to someone else. BulSU treats ID tampering as a serious offense. If security catches a tampered ID at the gate, the guard will confiscate it on the spot and escort you directly to your College Dean for disciplinary action.
