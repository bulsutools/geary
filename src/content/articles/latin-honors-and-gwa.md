Graduation honors at BulSU depend on three checks: your final General Weighted Average (GWA), your lowest grade in any single subject, and whether you carried enough units and residency semesters at the university.

> The Registrar and Academic Council determine final honors eligibility based on the handbook edition governing your admission batch. Confirm edge cases with your College Dean before applying for graduation.

## Latin honors GWA brackets

| GWA Range | Degree Courses | Non-Degree Courses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `1.00 to 1.20` | Summa Cum Laude | With Highest Honors |
| `1.21 to 1.45` | Magna Cum Laude | With High Honors |
| `1.46 to 1.75` | Cum Laude | With Honors |

## Residency, unit load, and grade disqualifiers

Meeting the GWA cutoff alone does not qualify you for graduation honors. Under Part II, Section Q of the Student Manual, candidates must also satisfy all of these conditions:

- No grade lower than `2.0` (or its equivalent) in any subject.
- At least **75%** of the program's total academic units completed at BulSU.
- At least **2 years** of continuous residency at BulSU.
- At least **15 credit units** prescribed in the curriculum taken every semester or term.

Only two situations exempt a student from the 15-unit semester minimum: when the curriculum itself prescribes fewer than 15 units in that term, or when the subjects needed to reach 15 units are not offered that semester.

## Gold Gear Academic Excellence Awards

Each school year during Recognition Day, the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) and academic units award Gold Gear citations to **second-year through senior students**. Eligibility covers your previous two consecutive semesters (the second semester of the prior academic year and the first semester of the current academic year) with at least **15 academic units** or your regular curriculum load, and no grade lower than `2.0`:

- **President's List Award:** GPA between `1.00 and 1.20`.
- **Dean's List Award:** GPA between `1.21 and 1.75`.

Gold Gear also grants **Competition Winners Awards** (1st to 5th place international, 1st to 3rd national, 1st to 2nd regional, and 1st place provincial or division) and **Extra-Curricular Awards** for Journalists of the Year, Male and Female Athletes, Performing Artists, Student Government Leaders, and Student Organization Leaders who have no disciplinary record during the current academic year.

## How GWA is calculated

Multiply each subject's final numerical grade by its academic unit weight, add those products across all graded subjects counted in your curriculum evaluation, then divide by the total number of academic units.
