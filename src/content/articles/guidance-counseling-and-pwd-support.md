The **Guidance and Counseling Center (`GCS`)** supports academic adjustment, mental health, career direction, and year-level testing, while a university committee coordinates accommodations for **Students with Special Needs and Persons with Disabilities (`SWSN`/`PWDs`)** under `RA 7277`.

## Guidance services and year-level tests

Students may visit `GCS` voluntarily or through faculty or parent referral for confidential individual or group counseling, cumulative inventory updates, career information, and referrals. Students placed on academic probation or earning low grades also receive individual follow-up sessions to help manage course loads ([Academic delinquency rules](/articles/academic-delinquency-and-probation)).

Undergraduate students take scheduled guidance assessments across year levels alongside scholarship, graduate admission, and clerical placement exams:

| Year Level | Required Guidance Test |
| :--- | :--- |
| College Freshmen | Mental Ability Test (`MAT`) |
| Second-Year Students | College Adjustment Scale (`CAS`) |
| Third-Year Students | `OMNI` Personality Inventory |

## Support for Students with Special Needs and PWDs

Under `RA 7277`, the BulSU `SWSN`/`PWD` Committee (chaired by the Director of Student Services alongside Medical, Guidance, Building and Grounds, and `PMO` representatives) tracks students from admission through graduation:

1. Identify needs during BSUAT admission and enrollment profiling.
2. Coordinate directly with professors, parents, and guardians to set tailored **academic accommodations**, physical building access, counseling referrals, and life-skills support.
3. Monitor progress through regular check-ins and parent conferences until program completion.
