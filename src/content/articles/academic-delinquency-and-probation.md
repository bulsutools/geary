Under Part II, Section M of the Student Manual, failing or dropping multiple subjects triggers a progressive sequence from a Dean's warning up to probation, forced shifting, or university disqualification. Maximum residency periods remain in effect throughout any probationary status.

## Delinquency escalation thresholds

| Standing Trigger | Consequence and Recovery Options |
| :--- | :--- |
| `1` failing grade | **Warning** issued by the College Dean. |
| `2` dropped or failed subjects | Barred from enrolling in requisite subjects next semester. You may take minor subjects in advance with Dean approval, up to your curriculum's unit limit. |
| `3` failed subjects (`< 50%` of enrolled subjects) | Placed on **probation** next semester with a commensurate reduction in unit load set by your unit head. |
| `2` dropped or failed subjects **while on probation** | **Dropped from the course.** You may apply to shift to another program with the receiving Dean's acceptance and `VPAA` approval. |
| Failing `50% to 75%` of enrolled subjects | Advised to **shift to another course** upon approval by the receiving Dean and `VPAA`. |
| Failing `> 75%` of enrolled subjects, OR failing `>= 50%` **while on probation** | **Disqualified** from continuing studies at Bulacan State University. |

## Shifting or returning while on probation

Students placed on probation receive follow-up monitoring through the [Guidance and Counseling Center](/articles/guidance-counseling-and-pwd-support) to help manage their reduced unit load. If you are advised or required to shift into another course, admission is never automatic: both the receiving College Dean and the Vice President for Academic Affairs must approve your transfer before enrollment.

Students disqualified from the university may still claim their [Transfer Credentials (Honorable Dismissal)](/articles/requesting-tor-and-transfer-credentials) at the Office of the Registrar once financial and property clearances are settled.
