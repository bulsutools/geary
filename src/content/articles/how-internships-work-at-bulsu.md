At BulSU, two campus offices handle your transition from classroom to workplace: the **Career Development and Student Internship (CarDSI)** office manages undergraduate internships (with International Affairs overseeing overseas internships under CMO No. 22, s.2013), and the **Placement and Public Employment Service Office (PPESO)** connects graduating students and alumni with employers.

## How Undergraduate Internships Work (CarDSI)

Schedules vary by college, and some programs begin practicum placement as early as the summer before third year. Follow this official placement sequence with your college's **Student Internship Program (SIP) Coordinator**:

1. **Attend the internship orientation.** Learn your college's specific practicum requirements, work ethics guidelines, and placement rules.
2. **Submit the Student-Intern Information Sheet.** Fill out all details accurately.
3. **Choose three potential Host Training Establishments (HTEs).** Pick three from the approved HTE list. If a company you want is missing from the list, ask your SIP Coordinator to vet its acceptability and arrange a partnership first.
4. **Contact HTEs and book an interview.** Reach out to the approved agencies to confirm internship slots and schedule your interview.
5. **Sign all placement documents.** Once you and your SIP Coordinator confirm your final HTE, the agreement documents must be signed by **you, the agency representative, the SIP Coordinator, and your parent**. Only then is placement official.

**Warning:** Skipping any part of this placement sequence results in **automatic de-registration** from your internship course. Misbehavior or defying campus or company standards can also lead CarDSI to withdraw you from the site and file disciplinary sanctions.

## What You Must Do During Your Practicum

Once placed, the manual expects every BulSU intern to follow eight core practices:

- Contact your site supervisor a few days before your start date.
- Notify your SIP Coordinator the day you begin logging hours.
- Attend any host-site orientation required.
- Maintain an **analytical field learning journal**.
- Stay in regular contact with your SIP Coordinator.
- Attend required campus meetings or seminars.
- Arrange for your SIP Coordinator to visit your host site.
- Wrap up professionally with a formal goodbye, thank-you, and clearance check with your SIP Coordinator.

## PPESO Career and Job Placement Services

Once you approach graduation or finish your degree, **PPESO** (operating with DOLE, LGUs, NGOs, and industry partners) provides free employment support:

- **Jobs Fairs and Referrals:** On-campus job matching events and pre-screened employer referrals for local vacancies (note: for overseas jobs, PPESO is barred from direct placement and refers applicants to licensed POEA channels).
- **Special Employment Programs:** Includes **SPES** (Special Program for Employment of Students during summer and Christmas breaks), **TULAY** (training and employment access for Persons with Disabilities), and **WHIP** (local DPWH infrastructure hiring).
- **Career Counseling and Seminars:** Employability enhancement seminars, labor market briefings, and pre-employment counseling alongside CarDSI and Guidance Services.
