When you decide to transfer from Bulacan State University to another college or university, your next school will require official transfer credentials (commonly including your Honorable Dismissal and academic transcript) from the Office of the Registrar. Note for newly admitted freshmen: under the BulSU Freshman Undergraduate Admissions Policy, students who have been admitted and enrolled in a program can only be granted an honorable dismissal once the semester officially starts.

## Clearance Comes First

To request your transfer credentials, you must clear every pending accountability on campus:

1. **Secure and accomplish a Clearance Form** covering your college, library, laboratories, student organization property (if an officer), and finance accounts.
2. **Settle any financial obligations** at the Cashier's Office or Finance Office.
3. **Submit the signed Clearance Form to the Office of the Registrar** along with any official receipt required for your credential copies.

Just like other official university records, BulSU honors the 30-day issuance timeframe counted from your completed request, provided no unpaid balance or pending disciplinary case places a hold on your records.

## What If You Were Dismissed or Faced Academic Delinquency?

Even if you were dismissed or academically disqualified from continuing your program at BulSU, you retain the right to leave and obtain your transfer credentials. Under the Student Manual:

- A dismissed student must still be **issued transfer credentials** once all financial obligations and university accountabilities are cleared.
- Likewise, students facing academic delinquency, illness, or rule violations cannot be blocked from transferring to another learning institution once clearances are satisfied.
