Knowing how academic load rules, prerequisite blocks, petitioned classes, and maximum residency interact helps you avoid delays on your way to graduation.

## Student Load Categories

- **Regular student:** Carries the full academic load set for a given semester in the curriculum.
- **Irregular student:** Enrolled for formal academic credits while carrying less than the full semester load.
- **Working student:** Holds a part-time job while studying and may enroll in a minimum of **15 academic units** in a term or semester.
- **Special student:** Enrolls without earning academic credits (maximum of **9 units** per semester for up to **2 academic years**, graded only as *Satisfactory* or *Unsatisfactory*; exemptions require unit head recommendation and VPAA approval).

## How Prerequisites Block Enrollment

Two rules in the manual stop you from enrolling in advanced subjects:

1. **Incomplete (`Inc.`) prerequisite:** If you receive `Inc.` in a prerequisite subject, you cannot enroll in the next subject that requires it until you finish and clear that `Inc.` grade.
2. **Two failures or drops:** Any student who drops or fails **two (2) subjects** in a term cannot enroll in requisite subjects the next semester. With your Dean's approval, you may take minor subjects ahead as long as you stay within your curriculum's unit limit.

## What If a Subject Is Not Offered? (Tutorial and Special Classes)

When a required subject is missing from the semester schedule, BulSU offers two workarounds:

### Tutorial Classes (Graduating Seniors Only)

- Open strictly to **senior-level students** when that single remaining subject will let you graduate that term.
- You must first get a **certification from the Office of the Registrar** confirming your graduating status.
- Recommended by your College or Campus Dean and approved by the **Vice President for Academic Affairs (VPAA)** on the main campus or **Vice President for Executive Operations** on satellite campuses.

### Special Classes (Irregular Students)

- May be requested by any irregular student who wants to **repeat** a subject or **take a subject in advance**, if it is not offered this semester or conflicts with your class schedule.
- Requires a **minimum of ten (10) requesting students** for the same subject and approval from your College or Campus Dean.
- The instructor's honorarium is charged to the requesting students.

## Maximum Residency Requirement

Because BulSU operates as a government-subsidized institution, you are expected to finish within the prescribed curriculum length. If delayed, your **maximum allowable completion period** is:

- **2-year course:** 4 years maximum
- **4-year course:** 6 years maximum
- **5-year course:** 7.5 years maximum

Three key residency rules apply:

- **Approved Leave of Absence:** Official LOA terms are excluded from your residency count.
- **Working student exemption:** Working students may be granted an extension upon recommendation by the Dean and approval by the VPAA.
- **What happens if you exceed the limit:** You may still continue and finish your degree, though your government tuition subsidy is forfeited and you must pay full tuition rates set by the Finance Office.

Planning to pause for a semester? File properly via [How to File a Leave of Absence](/articles/how-to-file-a-leave-of-absence).
