Need quiet space for research, references for a paper, or a novel between classes? Under Part V, Section B of the Student Manual, Bulacan State University Library maintains physical print collections, multimedia materials, OPAC terminals, and online databases alongside librarian assistance.

## What You Can Borrow and For How Long

Each section of the library follows different take-home and room-use rules:

| Collection | Take-Home / Use Rule | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Circulation Books** (Foreign & Filipiniana) | Overnight borrowing | Allowed when multiple copies exist or demand is low. |
| **Fiction Books** | 1 week (renewable twice) | Renewals allowed unless another reader placed a reservation. |
| **Reserve Section** | Room use only | Photocopying allowed following approved library policy. |
| **Reference Books** | Room use or photocopy only | Dictionaries, encyclopedias, and core references stay inside. |
| **Periodicals** | Room use or photocopy only | Maximum of **3 titles at a time**. |
| **Theses & Dissertations** | Room use only | Check in-room for capstone and thesis literature reviews. |
| **Cartographic & Audio-Visual Materials** | Room use (or classroom) | May be borrowed for classroom use with a **written request letter filed at least 1 day ahead**. |

## Library Services Available to Students

Beyond book checkouts, BulSU librarians offer free research and study support:

- **Library Orientation & Tours:** Walk-throughs on library rules, book borrowing, catalog systems, and basic references.
- **Database & OPAC Searching:** Guided searches across BulSU's Library OPAC, CD-ROM databases, and online internet databases.
- **One-to-One Research Instruction:** Personal help learning catalog systems and specialized reference tools when starting unfamiliar research.
- **Ready Reference & Bibliographic Verification:** Fast lookup help or verifying exact publication facts.
- **Current Awareness (CAS) & Reader's Advisory:** Help staying updated on new materials in your field and choosing books tailored to your interests.
- **Information and Referral (I & R):** Connecting you to external archives or partner libraries when BulSU lacks a specialized source.

Make sure you settle all library clearances at the end of every term or before graduation; unpaid library fines or unreturned books will block your clearance for [TOR and certificate requests](/articles/how-to-request-transcript-and-certificates) or [graduation](/articles/bulsu-graduation-requirements).
