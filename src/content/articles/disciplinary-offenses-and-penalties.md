Disciplinary action at BulSU escalates across seven sanction levels depending on offense severity and repeat violations: **Restitution**, **Reprimand** (verbal or written), **Withholding of academic record or degree**, **Suspension**, **Dismissal** (at least one year, decided by the University President), **Expulsion** (permanent ban with notation on your `TOR`, decided by the President), and **Transformative Experience (`TE`)** scheduled during vacant hours without cutting classes.

## The 16 offenses in the Student Manual

| Category | Handbook Items Covered | Escalation Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Light offenses (`1 to 4`) | Littering and unauthorized flyers; vandalism or unauthorized posting; class or campus disruption; unauthorized ticket or fund solicitation. | Progressive reprimand and `TE`; a **4th violation** across items `1 to 9` carries a **2-week suspension**. |
| Less grave offenses (`5 to 9`) | Smoking, gambling, or alcohol on campus; malicious accusation; deception, impersonation, or fraud; disrespect or refusal to obey university officials; property damage or unauthorized after-hours stay. | Higher `TE` hours, restitution for damage, and suspension; cumulative **4th violation** across items `1 to 9` carries a **2-week suspension**. |
| Grave offenses (`10 to 14`) | Theft or unauthorized possession; indecency or lewd acts; physical, verbal, sexual, cyber abuse, or hazing; illegal drugs; firearms and deadly weapons. | **4th violation** across items `10 to 11` leads to **Dismissal**. Serious injury or death from hazing or assault carries **Expulsion even on a 1st offense**. Illegal drug and weapon cases involve immediate **police turnover**. |

## Academic dishonesty and falsification

| Offense | 1st Offense | 2nd Offense | 3rd Offense |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cheating** (`15.1`) | Verbal reprimand, guidance referral, and zero on exam or output. | Written reprimand, failing grade, and `16` hours of `TE`. | **1-month suspension** and failing grade in the subject. |
| **Plagiarism** (`15.2`) | Failing score on the submitted output. | Failing grade in the subject and **2-week suspension**. | Failing grade in the subject and **1-semester suspension**. |
| **Falsification / Forging Records** (`16`) | **1-semester suspension**. | **1-year suspension**. | **Dismissal** from the university. |

## Multiple violations and 3-year expungement

Committing multiple distinct infractions accelerates your standing: **three different violations** count as the equivalent of a **2nd offense** of the gravest infraction committed, and **four different violations** count as a **3rd offense**.

Any current or former student who received a suspension of **one semester or longer** may petition the **University President** to expunge that penalty from permanent Registrar records **3 years after the finding**, weighed against offense severity, your full record, and subsequent good conduct.
