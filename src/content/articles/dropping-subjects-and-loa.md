Changing your schedule, dropping a class, or pausing enrollment for a semester requires written paperwork at the Registrar's Office. Simply walking away from a class results in an automatic `FDA` or `UD` mark on your record.

## Adding or changing subjects

You may add or swap enrolled subjects only during the **first two weeks of regular classes**, with approval from your academic unit head (Department Chair or Dean). If a required course from an older curriculum is no longer offered, see [Tutorial, Special Classes & Subject Substitution](/articles/tutorial-special-classes-and-substitution).

## Official dropping deadline and tuition refunds

To drop a subject officially, submit a dropping form signed by your instructor, College Dean, and the University Registrar **at least one week before midterm examinations** on the University calendar.

Students who paid tuition and withdraw or drop follow this refund schedule:

| Time of Withdrawal or Drop | Tuition Fee Charge & Refund |
| :--- | :--- |
| Before the scheduled opening of classes | Full refund of fees paid, minus the registration fee. |
| Within Week 1 of classes | `30%` of total tuition charged; balance refunded. |
| Within Week 2 of classes | `50%` of total tuition charged; balance refunded. |
| Within Week 3 of classes | `70%` of total tuition charged; balance refunded. |
| After Week 3 of classes | `100%` charged (no refund). |
| Stopped upon University Physician's advice | `100%` refund of tuition fees paid. |

## Leave of Absence (LOA) rules

An official LOA pauses your studies for up to **one year** without harming your academic standing, and approved LOA semesters do not count against your Maximum Residency limit. If you stay away longer than one year without extending or returning, your College Dean may require **3 to 6 additional academic units** upon readmission.

### Filing for an LOA

1. Pick up an **LOA Form** from the Office of the Registrar.
2. Collect every signature and clearance listed on the form.
3. Submit the finished form back to the Registrar's Office. Bring a photocopy and ask the receiving clerk to print their full name and sign it as your copy.

### Returning from an LOA

1. Visit the **Office of the Registrar** in person to request readmission, bringing your signed LOA photocopy to speed up retrieval.
2. Claim your **Readmission Slip** from the Registrar.
3. Present the Readmission Slip directly to your **College Dean** or authorized representative so you can enroll.

Returning penultimate and senior students stay under their old curriculum (using subject substitution when older courses are gone). Returning second-year students in 4-year programs, or third-year students in 5-year programs, move into the new curriculum.
