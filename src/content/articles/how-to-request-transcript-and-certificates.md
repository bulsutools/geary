The Office of the Registrar handles student academic records across BulSU, issuing your Official Transcript of Records (TOR), diploma, transfer credentials, Certificate of Grades (COG), and other official academic certifications.

## How Long Does Processing Take?

Under the **Education Act of 1982**, students hold an outer legal guarantee of **30 days** from request for official school documents. In practice, BulSU's **Joint Memorandum No. 14, Series of 2025** sets much faster standard working-day turnarounds once complete requirements are received on-site at the Office of the Registrar:

| Document Requested | Standard Turnaround |
| --- | --- |
| **CAV, Certified True Copy (TOR / Diploma), Certifications not requiring clearance** | **2 working days** |
| **TOR (2008 graduates to present), Certification of Grades (COG), Form 137, Course Descriptions, Certifications requiring clearance, RLE & Undertaking Cert, Duplicate Diploma** | **6 working days** |
| **TOR (2007 graduates and earlier)** | **9 working days** |

Remember too that BulSU can temporarily withhold academic records or a degree only if you owe an unpaid university debt or have a pending disciplinary case awaiting final disposition. Note from campus Registrar advisories (such as Sarmiento Campus, January 2026): if a system-generated balance blocks your **COR** while awaiting Main Campus Accounting adjustment, you can temporarily use your **Advising Slip** for scholarship documentation. Once you settle any balance or the case concludes, the hold ends. For graduating students, **"Graduated" TOR** releases also require completing every graduation requirement and submitting your Application for Graduation (graduates with record discrepancies are notified via email).

## How to Request Your Official Transcript of Records (TOR)

Whether you are an active student or graduating senior, follow these three requirements:

1. **Settle all financial obligations and accountabilities** with the university.
2. **Complete a school Clearance Form** signed by the required offices showing no pending property, library, or cash obligations.
3. **Pay the document fee** and present your completed Clearance Form together with the Official Receipt at the Registrar's Office.

For admission into second-degree or Certificate of Professional Teacher Education (CPTE) programs, your TOR also works as a core admission credential.

## Certificate of Grades (COG) and Certifications

Every enrolled student has the right to receive a copy of their **Certificate of Grades (COG)** at the end of every semester or term on the scheduled release date. You also retain the right to inspect and re-evaluate your class standing before grades are posted, and request grade corrections through your instructor and College Dean if a clerical error occurred.

For official certifications such as enrollment verification, graduation certifications, or copies of your diploma, request through the Office of the Registrar using your cleared account and official receipt. Remember to keep your Certificate of Registration (COR) safely too, since guards accept it for temporary gate entry if you ever misplace your ID.

## How to Update Your Name in University Records

The student manual outlines two formal paths depending on why your record needs a name fix:

- **Female students who married while enrolled at BulSU:** File an Affidavit of Change of Status together with an NSO-authenticated copy of your Marriage Contract at the Office of the Registrar.
- **Name discrepancies on admission or birth records:** If your school records or forms differ from your NSO birth certificate, you must submit a notarized Affidavit of Discrepancy alongside your credentials.
