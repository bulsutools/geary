Recognized organizations must clear activity letters, room requests, campus posters, fundraising permits, and peaceful assemblies through **OSOA** and university signatories before running any event.

## Activity permits and signatory chains

Prepare communication letters in **triplicate** together with a Compliance Checklist. Required final approval depends on scope and location:

| Activity Scope | Routing and Approving Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| University-wide, on-campus | Endorsed to the **Vice President for Academic Affairs (`VPAA`)**. |
| University-wide, off-campus | Endorsed to the **University President**. |
| College-based, on-campus | College Dean, then `OSOA` / `OSD` / `OSAS`, up to **`VPAA`**. |
| Satellite campus, on-campus | **Campus Administrator**. |
| Satellite campus, off-campus | Campus Administrator through `VPAA` to the **University President**. |

## Reserving classrooms and venues

Instructional classes take strict priority over organization bookings. Apply **at least 1 week before** your event, confirm room availability with the academic unit head before seeking `OSOA` clearance, and never lend or transfer an approved room booking to another organization.

## Campus posters and bulletin board rules

1. Submit a letter of intent plus an original sample of your poster or tarpaulin to `OSOA` for clearance.
2. Secure College Dean approval before mounting materials on college walls or boards.
3. Attach items using **scotch tape, masking tape, or push pins on corkboards only**. Paint, chalk, and permanent glue are prohibited.
4. Keep posted displays up for at most **2 weeks** (renewable upon request), and remove all materials **the day after expiry**.

Off-campus political candidates and partisan campaign issues may not be posted on campus bulletin boards.

## Fundraising limits and financial reports

Each recognized organization is allowed at most **one major fundraising activity per academic year**, requiring a permit from the **University President** plus a **`DSWD` permit** if held off-campus.

Submit your post-event financial report to `OSOA` **within 2 weeks after the activity**. `Reimbursement Expense Receipts` are capped at **`P1,000`**, and treasurers should **photocopy thermal paper receipts immediately** before the ink fades.

## Peaceful student assemblies

Groups planning a campus assembly or rally must file a written request with the **University President at least 2 school days before holding or announcing the event**. Students raising grievances should pursue dialogue or a position paper first. During approved assemblies, organizers must keep campus gates and roads unblocked, bar alcohol, drugs, and weapons, and disperse on time.
