Formal disciplinary complaints (outside Student Publication cases) are heard at the college or satellite level by the **Student Discipline Committee** before reaching the university-wide **Board of Student Discipline (`BSD`)**.

## Who hears the case

The **Student Discipline Committee** is chaired by the College Secretary (or Lab High School Principal) alongside the local faculty president, student governor, PTA president, a faculty secretary, and a non-voting arbiter with legal training (who steps in as voting alternate Chair when needed).

Decisions and penalties are issued by the **Board of Student Discipline (`BSD`)**, made up of the Dean of OSAS (Chair), the concerned College Dean, the Student Government President, legal counsel, and a board secretary.

## Formal hearing timeline

1. **Sworn complaint:** The complainant files a sworn written statement. If no *prima facie* case exists, the complaint is dismissed outright.
2. **Written answer (`5 working days`):** If a *prima facie* case exists, the respondent receives written notice and has **5 working days** to file a sworn answer.
3. **Hearing convened (`5 working days`):** Hearings open within **5 working days** in a closed, informal, non-litigious setting. Parties pass questions through the Committee, may retain legal counsel at their own expense, and absence does not equal an admission of guilt.
4. **Recommendation and decision (`5 + 5 working days`):** The Committee forwards its findings and recommended penalty within **5 working days**, and `BSD` issues its decision within **5 working days** of receipt.

## Summary proceedings (no formal hearing)

The Committee or `BSD` skips a full hearing and proceeds summarily when any of these conditions apply:

- The respondent admits guilt in writing.
- The respondent was caught in the act.
- Strong documentary or physical evidence proves the violation on its face.
- The respondent is a habitual offender with **three or more prior punishments**.

## Reconsideration and 10-day appeals

If you disagree with a `BSD` penalty, file a **Motion for Reconsideration** with the `BSD` within **10 working days** of receiving the ruling. A `BSD` denial is final and executory unless the imposed penalty is **Dismissal or Expulsion**, in which case you may appeal directly to the **University President** or **Board of Regents (`BOR`)** within **10 working days**.
