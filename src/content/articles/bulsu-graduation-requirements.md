Finishing every class on your curriculum checklist is the biggest hurdle, though clearing BulSU's administrative steps is what puts your name on the commencement list. Make sure every item below is cleared ahead of time.

## Four Mandatory Graduation Requirements

Under Part II, Section O of the Student Manual, every candidate for graduation must satisfy four conditions:

1. **Apply for graduation:** File the official **Application for Graduation** form at the Office of the Registrar once you have satisfied all requirements of your program.
2. **One-year residency minimum:** Complete at least **one (1) year of residence** at BulSU prior to your graduation date (note: graduating with Latin Honors requires two years of residency).
3. **Full clearance:** Clear every property and financial accountability across the university (library, laboratories, organizations, and Cashier) before commencement exercises.
4. **Zero pending discipline issues:** All disciplinary charges against you must be resolved and any assigned sanctions fully completed before you are eligible to graduate.

## Final-Semester Pitfalls: Incomplete Grades and Missing One Subject

- **If you have an `Inc.` or `4.00`:** Because you must satisfy all requirements of your course to apply for graduation, any unresolved `Inc.` or `4.00` blocks clearance. Worse, an uncompleted `4.00` flips to `5.00` after 2 weeks, and an unresolved `Inc.` flips to `5.00` after 1 year.
- **If only one unoffered subject blocks you from graduating:** As a senior, you may request a **Tutorial Class** if that single subject means you can finish your degree that term. Get a graduating-student certification from the Registrar, obtain your Dean's endorsement, and get approval from the VPAA (Main Campus) or Vice President for Executive Operations (satellite campuses).

## Commencement Exercises and Loyalty Award

- **Attendance and academic costume:** Candidates for graduation are enjoined to attend their academic unit's commencement exercises (dates set by the Academic Council) and **must wear the prescribed academic costume** for both the traditional baccalaureate service and commencement ceremonies.
- **Loyalty Award:** Given at graduation to any student who started Grade 7 at BulSU (Laboratory High School) and completed their bachelor's degree continuously at BulSU.

Once you clear graduation, see [How to Request Your TOR and Certificates](/articles/how-to-request-transcript-and-certificates) for your 30-day issuance right on diplomas and transcripts, or check [Can I Graduate With Honors?](/articles/can-i-graduate-with-honors) for Latin Honors and Gold Gear rules.
