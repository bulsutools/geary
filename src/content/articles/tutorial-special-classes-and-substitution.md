When a subject is not on the semester schedule, conflicts with another required class, or no longer exists under a revised curriculum, the Student Manual provides three routes to keep you on track. All schedule adjustments must still be settled within the first **two weeks of regular classes** ([dropping and load changes](/articles/dropping-subjects-and-loa)).

## Tutorial classes (graduating seniors only)

A tutorial class lets one student complete an unoffered subject through guided study, strictly when **only that single subject separates you from graduating that term**.

1. Request certification from the **Office of the Registrar** confirming your graduating status and that the requested subject determines your graduation.
2. Secure a favorable recommendation from your **College or Campus Dean**.
3. Submit the endorsed request for final approval by the **Vice President for Academic Affairs (`VPAA`)** on the Main Campus, or the **Vice President for External Operations (`VPEO`)** on satellite campuses.

## Special classes (10-student minimum)

Irregular students who need to repeat a failed course or take a subject not offered during the term (or blocked by a schedule conflict) may petition for a special class:

- At least **10 requesting students** must sign on for the same subject.
- The request must be approved by the **College or Campus Dean**.
- **The instructor's honorarium is charged directly to the requesting students**, divided among the group.

## Subject substitution

If a subject listed under your older curriculum has been phased out after a curriculum revision, you may apply for subject substitution when all three rules apply:

- The original subject came from an old or replaced curriculum and is no longer offered.
- The proposed substitute covers related or similar subject matter.
- The substitute carries **equal or greater credit units** than the replaced subject.
