BulSU grades undergraduate courses on a `1.00` to `5.00` scale, where `3.00` is the lowest passing mark. Special status marks handle conditional passes, incomplete requirements, and dropped subjects.

## Numerical grade equivalents

| Grade / Mark | Meaning / Percentage Equivalent |
| :--- | :--- |
| `1.00` | `97-100%` |
| `1.25` | `94-96%` |
| `1.50` | `91-93%` |
| `1.75` | `88-90%` |
| `2.00` | `85-87%` |
| `2.25` | `82-84%` |
| `2.50` | `79-81%` |
| `2.75` | `76-78%` |
| `3.00` | `75%` (Lowest Passing Grade) |
| `4.00` | Conditional Passed |
| `5.00` | Failed |
| `Inc.` | Incomplete |
| `D` | Officially Dropped |
| `FDA` / `UD` | Failure Due to Absences / Unofficially Dropped |

## Conditional Passed (4.00) and Incomplete (Inc.)

Both `4.00` and `Inc.` are temporary marks that automatically turn into **`5.00`** if you miss the deadline to settle them.

| Rule | `4.00` (Conditional Passed) | `Inc.` (Incomplete) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reason given** | Slight deficiency below the `3.00` passing standard. | Passing class standing overall, but missing a specific project or course requirement. |
| **Completion deadline** | **2 weeks** from receiving the grade. | **1 year** from the term the mark was given. |
| **If unresolved** | Automatically becomes `5.00`. | Automatically becomes `5.00`. |
| **Transcript record** | Does not appear on permanent records or TOR once resolved. | Stays on record until completed and blocks prerequisite subjects. |

If you get an `Inc.` in a prerequisite course, you cannot enroll in the follow-up subject until you finish the missing requirement and clear the grade.

## Official and unofficial drops (D, FDA, UD)

You earn a `D` (Officially Dropped) only by filing a formal dropping form signed by your instructor, College Dean, and the University Registrar before the deadline. Stopping attendance without formal clearance, or passing the pre-midterm absence limit, results in `FDA` (Failure Due to Absences) or `UD` (Unofficially Dropped).

## Grade transparency and fixing posting errors

Before grades are encoded, you have the right to see how your grade was computed, inspect your class record, and know your final mark. At the end of every term, you are entitled to receive a Certificate of Grades (`COG`).

When an instructor spots a computation or encoding error after grades have already been posted, they cannot change the grade on their own. The faculty member must send a written request with supporting records to the College or Campus Dean, and the Dean forwards written approval to the Office of the Registrar for official correction.
