Finishing all curriculum subjects does not automatically place a student on the commencement list. Under Part II, Section P and Section Q of the Student Manual, graduating candidates must file an application, satisfy residency, and clear every financial and disciplinary accountability before graduation day.

## Graduation eligibility checklist

- File an official **Application for Graduation** form at the **Office of the Registrar**.
- Complete at least **one year of residence** at BulSU immediately prior to graduation (candidates aiming for Latin honors must meet the stricter [2-year and 75%-unit rule](/articles/latin-honors-and-gwa)).
- Settle all money and property accountabilities with university offices before commencement exercises.
- Resolve all pending disciplinary cases and finish any imposed sanctions.
- Attend baccalaureate services and commencement exercises wearing the prescribed academic costume.

## Special graduation awards beyond Latin honors

Alongside Summa, Magna, and Cum Laude distinctions, graduating students may be nominated for five university and college citations:

| Award | Basis of Selection |
| :--- | :--- |
| Outstanding Student Award | Combined academic standing, co-curricular and extra-curricular involvement, leadership, and good moral character. |
| Best in Specialization | Top academic performance and thesis or capstone excellence in a student's major field or professional track. |
| College or University Leadership Award | Active governance and service in recognized student organizations or Student Government, endorsed by peers and advisers. |
| Honorary Award | Conferred upon the graduating **Student Regent** in recognition of service on the Board of Regents. |
| Loyalty Award | Granted to graduates who studied continuously at BulSU from **Grade 7 through college graduation**. |
