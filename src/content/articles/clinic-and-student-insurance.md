Enrolled students can visit the **Medical and Dental Services** clinic for consultations and clearances, and carry **24/7 worldwide student accident insurance** paid through miscellaneous fees. Save all hospital bills, official receipts, prescriptions, and police reports right after any accident so OSW reimbursement is not delayed.

## Medical and dental clinic services

Staffed by a university physician, dentist, and nurses, the clinic handles general checkups, first aid, medicine dispensing, emergency ambulance transfers, oral prophylaxis, and tooth extractions. For tooth extractions, blood pressure is checked first; patients with high blood pressure need medical clearance from a physician before the dentist proceeds.

### Documents for clinic medical certificates

| Purpose of Certificate | Required Items |
| :--- | :--- |
| Excuse for class absence | Parent or guardian excuse letter plus a copy of the parent or guardian's ID for nurse and physician evaluation. |
| OJT or internship | Official Chest X-Ray plate and official radiologist reading for University Physician sign-off. |
| Employment clearance | Official Chest X-Ray with reading, CBC, Urinalysis, Fecalysis, and Drug Test results. |

## 24/7 Student Accident Insurance claims

Every student who pays miscellaneous fees is covered around the clock anywhere in the world for accidental injury or death.

### How to claim reimbursement through OSW

1. Go to the **Office of Student Welfare (OSW)** to pick up and fill out the **Accident Claim Checklist Form**.
2. Ask OSW for copies of the university **Insurance Policy** and the **Official Receipt of Premium Payment**.
3. Attach an official **Police Report or Accident Investigation Report**, along with endorsements if applicable.
4. Submit the matching medical or legal records below to OSW, then wait for notice to claim your payout check.

**Bodily injury claims** require a Medical Certificate, Hospital Statement of Account or billing summary, Official Receipts for both hospital charges and medicines, and original medical prescriptions. **Death claims** require a Death Certificate, Official Receipts for funeral or burial expenses, and a Medical Certificate if the student was hospitalized prior to passing.
