Changing degree programs inside BulSU or transferring in from another university follows strict grade cutoffs, exam rules, and credit transfer approvals through the Admissions Office, receiving College Dean, and Office of the Registrar.

## Shifting within BulSU

Continuing BulSU students in good standing may apply to shift programs subject to slot availability and academic performance:

- You must have satisfactory grades in your previous coursework.
- Moving between different degree programs (for example, from Education to Engineering) requires passing a **shifter's exam**.
- Changing majors **within the same degree program** exempts you from taking the shifter's exam.
- Students shifting from BulSU external campuses into the Main Campus, as well as ladderized students proceeding to a baccalaureate program, must take the corresponding shifter or continuing exam.

When filing at Admissions, present your Certificate of Registration (`COR`) and class cards, plus the **`P300.00`** examination fee receipt if your shift requires a test. See [Academic delinquency, probation, and disqualification](/articles/academic-delinquency-and-probation) if you are shifting after academic probation or dismissal from a course.

## Transferring from another college or university

Outside applicants are accepted only when program slots are available and all four academic thresholds are met:

- Cumulative GWA of **`2.5` or better** across all collegiate subjects previously taken.
- **No failing grade of `5.0`** (or its equivalent) in any subject.
- Completed **not more than `50%`** of the total units required by the target BulSU course.
- Passing score on the BSUAT or transfer qualifying assessment.

### Transfer documents and credit evaluation

Submit a completed application form, two `2"x2"` colored ID photos on a white background, your latest `COR` and class cards for initial evaluation, and your official **Transcript of Records (`TOR`)** and **Honorable Dismissal** for Registrar processing.

To have prior college units credited toward your BulSU curriculum, file a request for **transfer credit** at the Office of the Registrar. Evaluated subjects require approval from the **Vice President for Academic Affairs (`VPAA`)**.
