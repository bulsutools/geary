Stepping onto a BulSU campus for the first time comes with unwritten habits every upperclassman picks up fast, plus official rules every freshman signs off on. Here is what the student manual sets out for your first year.

## Handog ni BulSU kay Bunso Orientation

All incoming freshmen and transfer students are required to attend *Handog ni BulSU kay Bunso*, BulSU's annual orientation before classes open in the first semester. You'll meet university officials, tour academic offices and student services, and get walked through academic regulations and campus traditions. Even upperclassmen get an annual re-orientation each first semester to refresh policy rules.

You'll also find a duplicate **Acknowledgement Form** inside the Undergraduate Student Manual. Fill it out, have your parent or guardian sign it, and submit the duplicate copy to your College Secretary's office or campus. Signing confirms you know you are accountable for following manual policies even if you overlook a section later.

## Daily Essentials: Portal, IDs, and Uniform Days

Bookmark **`https://alabsms.bulsu.edu.ph/`** (**ALAB SMS**, which replaced the older HEIMS portal in late 2026) for viewing enrollments and submitting add/drop/change requests. If your login fails, file a helpdesk ticket at `https://bulsupport.zohodesk.com/portal/en/signin`.

At campus gates, guards run a strict **No ID, No Entry** policy, so wear your university ID at all times on campus. If you forget it at home, bring your Certificate of Registration (COR) to the gate guard, who will note the date and sign the back of your COR for temporary entry.

Plan your wardrobe around the weekly schedule (check your Local Student Council's A.Y. 2026-2027 guide for college pins or skirt/slacks specs):

- **Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays:** Prescribed college or campus daily uniform paired with black shoes (the 2016 manual lists Friday as daily uniform too unless wearing an org shirt).
- **Fridays:** Recognized student organization or college shirt (or your daily uniform).
- **Wednesdays, Saturdays, and Sundays:** Wash Day / Free Days (decent, non-provocative civilian attire).
- **PATHFIT / PE, NSTP, and Lab classes:** Wear those specific activity uniforms or lab outfits only during those class hours.

Read the full hair color, earrings, and grooming checklist in [BulSU Dress Code, Grooming, and ID Policy](/articles/bulsu-dress-code-and-id-policy).

## First-Year Health and Guidance Steps

Two campus offices expect every freshman early on:

- **BulSU Medical and Dental Services:** Freshmen, transferees, and shifters undergo an annual physical, medical, and dental examination. Free consultations, dental checks, and emergency care are open whenever you need them.
- **Guidance and Counseling Services Center:** Freshmen take the standardized **Mental Ability Test (MAT)** (followed by the College Adjustment Scale in 2nd year and OMNI Personality Inventory in 3rd year), and counseling is open anytime for academic adjustment or personal concerns.

## Your Student Classification Matters

In your Certificate of Registration and class lists, BulSU labels your status:

- **Regular student:** Carries the full academic load set for your semester and curriculum.
- **Irregular student:** Registered for credit but carrying less than a full load.
- **Working student:** Holds a part-time job and may enroll in a minimum of 15 academic units per semester (and can later apply for residency extensions if approved).
- **Special student:** Enrolls without earning academic credit (maximum 9 units per semester, up to 2 academic years, graded Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory).

Freshmen are also welcome to join recognized student organizations voluntarily right from your first year! Start exploring in [BulSU Student Organizations Guide](/articles/bulsu-student-organizations-guide) and check classroom tardiness rules in [Understanding University Attendance Policies](/articles/university-attendance-policies).
