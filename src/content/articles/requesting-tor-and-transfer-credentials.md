The **Office of the Registrar** manages student numbers, ID validation, Certificates of Grades (`COG`), Transcript of Records (`TOR`), diplomas, and transfer credentials. Under the Education Act of 1982 quoted in Part I of the Student Manual, students have the right to receive requested certificates, diplomas, `TOR`, and transfer credentials **within 30 days from filing**.

## Official Transcript of Records (TOR)

Before the Registrar can release an official `TOR`, you must clear every financial and property obligation owed to university offices.

1. Secure and complete a university **Clearance Form** signed by all required offices.
2. Pay the applicable processing fee at the **Cashier** and keep the **Official Receipt (`OR`)**.
3. Submit your accomplished clearance form and payment receipt to the **Office of the Registrar** to queue your `TOR` for printing and release.

## Transfer credentials (Honorable Dismissal)

If you are transferring to another school, submit a duly accomplished and signed **Clearance Form** directly to the Registrar's Office to process your Honorable Dismissal and academic transfer packet.

Under Part II, Section S of the Student Manual, even students dismissed for disciplinary or academic reasons retain the right to claim their transfer credentials once all financial and property accountabilities are cleared.

## Updating your name after marriage

Married female students who wish to update their official name on university records must submit two documents to the **Office of the Registrar**:

1. A notarized **Affidavit of Change of Status**.
2. An **NSO (PSA) copy of your Marriage Contract**.

At the end of every regular term, enrolled students may also claim their **Certificate of Grades (`COG`)** through the Registrar as confirmation of final encoded marks.
