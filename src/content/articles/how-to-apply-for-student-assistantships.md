If you want to earn extra income around your class schedule without leaving campus, BulSU offers paid student work through its **Institutional Student Assistant (ISA)** program (handled in 2026 calls by **BulSU PPESO**, Placement Office, Ground Floor, Roxas Hall, alongside the Student Manual's **Student Assistantship / OSFA** rules) plus seasonal break jobs through **SPES**.

## Institutional Student Assistant (ISA) Program (2026 Updates)

Per **BulSU PPESO**'s A.Y. 2026-2027 ISA call (July 2026), requirements differ slightly depending on whether you are renewing or applying fresh:

- **New ISA applicants:** Submit your accomplished **Application Form**, **Certificate of Registration (COR)**, **Certificate of Grades (COG)** from the previous semester, and **Updated Class Schedule** to the Placement Office (Ground Floor, Roxas Hall).
- **Returning / old ISAs:** Submit a **Recommendation Letter** for reappointment.
- **Key scheduling rules:** Slots are limited, so applicants with at least **four (4) hours of daily vacant time** are encouraged, duty follows a strict **"No class, no duty"** guideline, and **first-year students are encouraged to apply starting Second Semester**.

## Baseline Eligibility Rules (Student Manual)

Under Part IV, Section I.B.III of the Student Manual, Student Assistants earn wages at BulSU's student rate and must meet three baseline conditions:

1. **Clean previous semester:** You must have **no failing grade (`5.00`) and no incomplete mark (`Inc.`)** from the previous semester.
2. **Pass office screening:** You must clear the pre-hiring screening requirements set by the specific BulSU office requesting an assistant.
3. **Parental consent:** Submit a duly signed **parental consent** form if requested during screening.

Watch both **BulSU PPESO** and **OSFAS** announcements before each academic year for exact opening dates and window deadlines.

Because Student Assistants balance office hours with academics, make sure any work hours stay clear of your regular classes so you do not risk tardiness or automatic drops under [Understanding University Attendance Policies](/articles/university-attendance-policies). Remember too that working students holding part-time jobs may enroll in a minimum of **15 academic units** per semester.

## Seasonal Alternative: SPES During Breaks

If semester loads leave no room for regular campus work hours, ask **PPESO** (Placement and Public Employment Service Office) about **SPES (Special Program for Employment of Students)**, which connects financially deserving students with temporary jobs during summer and Christmas vacations.

Want to stack an institutional, government, or private tuition grant too? Check [Complete Guide to Scholarships at BulSU](/articles/complete-guide-to-scholarships-at-bulsu).
