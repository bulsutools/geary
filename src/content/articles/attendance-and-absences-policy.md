Only students on the official **MIS enrollment list** may attend regular classes; sitting in requires prior approval from the academic unit head. Under Part II, Section H and Section I of the Student Manual, strict minute cutoffs govern tardiness, professor no-shows, and pre-midterm automatic drops.

## Tardiness and late-arrival cutoffs

- Arriving **`15 minutes`** after the scheduled class start marks you **tardy**.
- Arriving **`20 minutes`** late marks you **absent**.

Even when marked tardy or absent for coming in late, an instructor may not bar you from staying to follow the lesson.

## How long to wait if an instructor is absent

Unless the instructor sent prior notice, wait according to the length of the scheduled period before leaving:

| Scheduled Class Duration | Required Wait Time |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1-hour class | `15 minutes` |
| 2-hour class | `30 minutes` |
| 3-hour class | `45 minutes` |

Students who leave the room before the wait period ends are marked absent even if the professor never arrives afterward.

## Excused absences and the 30% cap

Absences are excused only for **official university representation** (curricular, co-curricular, or extra-curricular), **certified illness** signed by the attending or University physician ([Clinic absence clearance](/articles/clinic-and-student-insurance)), or **force majeure**. An excused mark never waives coursework: you remain responsible for missed assignments and exams, and total excused absences are capped at **`30%`** of total semester class hours.

## Automatic drop for excessive unexcused absences

Accumulating **more than `20%` unexcused absences** in any subject before midterm examinations triggers an automatic drop recorded as **`FDA` (Failure Due to Absences)** or **`UD` (Unofficially Dropped)**.
