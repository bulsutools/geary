College brings personal, family, and academic pressure, and BulSU's **Guidance and Counseling Services Center (GCS)** exists so you never have to figure things out alone. You can walk in voluntarily, book an appointment, or be referred for free, student-centered support.

## Six Core Services at the Guidance Center

1. **Individual and Group Counseling:** A safe space to talk through stress, interpersonal concerns, anxiety, personal values, or big life choices with trained counselors.
2. **Standardized Testing & Interpretation:** Assessments to help gauge your strengths, aptitudes, interests, personality, and learning difficulties, with counselors explaining your results back to you:
   - **1st Year:** Mental Ability Test (MAT)
   - **2nd Year:** College Adjustment Scale (CAS)
   - **3rd Year:** OMNI Personality Inventory
   - Also administers tests for sponsored scholarships, Graduate School Admission Test, and teaching/clerical screenings.
3. **Academic Probation & Low-Grade Follow-Up:** If you end up on academic probation or earn low grades, GCS reaches out individually to pinpoint what caused the slip and build practical study habits and coping skills to handle your load.
4. **Information Services:** Print bulletins, career brochures, personal-social guides, symposiums, and seminar-workshops on campus rules and career choices.
5. **Individual Inventory:** Maintains your cumulative guidance profile (background, test results, and activities) to match you with relevant university programs.
6. **Specialist Referral Service:** Connects students needing specialized outside or campus help (for severe emotional distress, physical disability accommodations, financial distress, program misplacement, or family difficulties) to the right specialists.

## Support for Students With Special Needs and PWDs

Under Republic Act 7277, BulSU also runs a dedicated **Committee on Students with Special Needs and Persons with Disabilities (SWSN / PWDs)** chaired by the Director of Student Services alongside the heads of Medical Services, Guidance and Counseling, Building and Grounds Management, and the Project Management Office:

- Holds regular consultations with SWSN/PWD students, professors, parents or guardians, and personal assistants to arrange tailored **academic accommodations**.
- Provides counseling and testing referrals to track psychosocial adaptability, plus life-skills workshops like conflict management.
- Partners with the Safety and Security Office to check building accessibility and prioritize PWD assistance during emergency evacuations.
