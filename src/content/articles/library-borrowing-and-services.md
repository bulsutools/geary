What you can check out from the University Library and for how long depends on the collection type, from overnight circulation copies to room-use archival holdings.

## Borrowing rules by collection

| Collection Type | Loan & Access Rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| Circulation books (Foreign & Filipiniana) | Eligible for overnight loan when multiple copies exist and demand is low. |
| Reserve & Reference books | Room use and photocopying only. |
| Fiction books | **1 week** loan, renewable twice unless reserved by another borrower. |
| Periodicals | Room use and photocopying only; maximum of **3 titles** checked out at one time. |
| Theses & Dissertations | Strictly room use only. |
| Cartographic & Audio-Visual materials | Room use; classroom loan allowed with a written request submitted **at least 1 day ahead**. |

## Library search and research services

- **Catalog & database access:** Search holdings through `OPAC`, `CD-ROM`, and internet academic databases.
- **Reference & research help:** Request Ready Reference lookup, Bibliographic Verification, Current Awareness Service (`CAS`), Reader's Advisory, or Information and Referral (`I & R`).
- **Instruction:** Join Library Orientation sessions or ask library staff for one-to-one catalog instruction.
