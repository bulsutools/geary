The **Safety and Security Office** manages BulSU's four Main Campus gates (`Gates 1 to 4`), issues traffic and conduct violation slips, and coordinates disaster risk reduction (`DRRM`) evacuations across campus buildings.

## Earthquake response steps

1. Move immediately to a safe interior spot away from glass windows, tall shelves, and heavy fixtures.
2. **Drop, Cover, and Hold On** until the shaking completely stops.
3. Check yourself and nearby classmates for injuries while staying alert for aftershocks and secondary fires.
4. **Always use stairs and never use elevators** when leaving a building.
5. Stay outdoors in an open space away from walls, trees, streetlights, and power lines (fatalities and injuries often happen within **10 feet of building entrances and doorways** from falling debris).

## Fire response and evacuation rules

Stay calm and alert nearby occupants. Activate the building fire alarm **only if you are inside the burning building**; never pull a building alarm if you are standing outside. Call the University Emergency Hotline or local fire responders, **never lock doors** behind you while evacuating, and assist classmates with disabilities (`PWDs`) down safe routes.
