Freshman applications run through the **University Admissions Office (UAO)** online portal at `https://admissions.bulsu.edu.ph/` (`admissions@bulsu.edu.ph`) before you bring physical copies to campus on your appointment date. Application windows are posted on the **BulSU Admissions Office Facebook page** (`/BulSUAdmissionsOffice`), while final qualifier lists go up on the **BulSU website**.

For reference on timing, the **ATBulSU 2027** cycle opened online applications on August 25, 2026 with a November 26, 2026 online deadline and November 27, 2026 hard-copy deadline (while the prior 2026 cycle ran September 22 to December 19, 2025).

## Step-by-Step Freshman Application (ATBulSU)

1. **Open the admissions portal.** Check `/BulSUAdmissionsOffice` for current cycle announcements, then apply at `https://admissions.bulsu.edu.ph/` once you confirm you meet your target program's Grade 11 requirements in [BulSU Admission Requirements](/articles/bulsu-admission-requirements).
2. **Enter your combined Grade 11 ratings and pick your courses.** Per the **BulSU Student Government Academic & Admissions FAQ (Aug 30, 2026)**, encode your combined 1st and 2nd Semester Grade 11 ratings (per-quarter highest/lowest plus combined final average). Note that the portal automatically hides programs whose minimum grade rules your grades do not meet, and deceased-parent applicants should pick the form option matching their situation.
3. **Upload scans before your appointment.** Upload scanned copies of your sealed Grade 11 SF9, Declaration Form with `2" x 2"` photo, Barangay Proof of Residency, recent utility bill, ITR or Tax Exemption/Indigency certificate, plus any optional affirmative action documents.
4. **Submit hard copies at the exact campus where you applied.** Bring the physical documents matching your uploaded scans to your selected BulSU campus on your appointment date.
   - **No confirmation email?** Check spam/trash, inbox storage, or typos first. If you still got no email, bring a parent or guardian on appointment day to sign the Declaration on-site so your slot is not rescheduled.
   - **Emergency representative:** An authorized person may submit your hard copies on your behalf during emergencies if they carry a signed **authorization letter** plus the **valid ID** specified in that letter.
5. **Wait for your ATBulSU Test Permit email.** If qualified to take the exam, UAO emails your **ATBulSU Test Permit** listing your exam date, time, and venue. Print it out and bring it on test day.
6. **Prepare for college interviews or skill tests.** Once UAO ranks initial qualifiers, programs that require interviews, specialization exams (such as Engineering), skill checks, or talent auditions notify shortlisted applicants with the exact schedule.

## Choosing 1st and 2nd Program Options

When filling out your application, you choose up to two courses:

- **1st Choice Program:** Your top priority degree.
- **2nd Choice Program:** Must be chosen from **allied programs** connected to your 1st choice. If you miss your first-choice cut, evaluation continues automatically for your second choice.

## How BulSU Scores and Ranks Qualifiers

Evaluation uses a **100-point (100%)** scoring rubric combining your **ATBulSU** exam, Grade 11 scholastic record, SHS strand alignment, and **Affirmative Action** points.

### All Colleges (Except Engineering)

| Criterion | Programs With No Interview | Programs With Interviews |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ATBulSU Test Score** | 50% | 50% |
| **Grade 11 GWA (SF9)** | 15% | 10% |
| **SHS Strand Alignment** | 15% | 10% |
| **College Interview** | N/A | 10% |
| **Affirmative Action** | 20% | 20% |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100%** |

Strand alignment awards full points when your Grade 11 strand matches the degree directly (**Related**: `15` pts no interview / `10` pts with interview), fewer points for **Allied** strands (`10` / `7`), and baseline points for **Not Related** strands (`5` / `5`).

### College of Engineering (PTC-ACBET Alignment)

Engineering applicants take an extra **College of Engineering (COE) Exam** alongside ATBulSU:

| Criterion | Requirement | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ATBulSU Test Score** | Admission test result | **40%** |
| **COE Exam Score** | Engineering aptitude test | **20%** |
| **Grade 11 GWA** | School Form 9 (Form 138) | **10%** |
| **SHS Strand Taken** | STEM (`10`), GAS (`5`), other strands (`2`) | **10%** |
| **Affirmative Action** | Personal & socio-economic criteria | **20%** |
| **Total** | | **100%** |

**Licensure-board exam floor:** Across board courses leading to PRC licensure exams, applicants must obtain **80% or better** in the admission examination.

## Mistakes That Disqualify Your Application

Any one of these five errors voids your application outright:

1. Misrepresenting facts anywhere on the portal or submitted forms
2. Violating application instructions
3. Failing to submit hard-copy documents on your scheduled date
4. **Creating multiple applications**
5. Falsifying the Declaration Form

If you are already enrolled in another college or another BulSU course, **do not apply through Freshman Admission** because transferees and shifters caught using the freshman flow are barred from all BulSU programs. Once admitted and enrolled, incoming students can also only obtain an **honorable dismissal once the semester starts**.

After qualifiers are posted on the **BulSU website**, follow the posted slot reservation and enrollment dates strictly, then read [What Every BulSU Freshman Should Know](/articles/essential-things-freshmen-should-know) to prepare for *Handog ni BulSU kay Bunso* orientation and campus entry.
