If you want to compete for BulSU in athletics, music, dance, theater, or academic contests, two offices guide student teams: the **Sports Development Office** (under the College of Physical Education, Recreation and Sports) and the **Cultural and Arts Affairs Office** (under Student Development).

## Two Ways to Join BulSU Varsity Teams

1. **Outside recruitment (incoming athletes):** You may be recruited before entry if you finished as at least a **bronze winner** in Palarong Pambansa or an equivalent national meet, a **silver winner** in regional competition, or a **gold winner** in provincial competition. (Division and district winners do not qualify under external recruitment.)
2. **Campus scout, draft, or tryouts:** Enrolled BulSUans get noticed by coaches during **University Intramurals** or open team tryouts.

Coaches evaluate athlete candidates using five weighted criteria:

- **Attitude:** 30%
- **Level of competition:** 20%
- **Sports skills:** 20%
- **Academic grades:** 20%
- **Age:** 10%

Varsity members commit to year-round, semestral, and summer training clinics. Deserving athletes competing in meets like **SCUAA** and **UCLAA** receive **50% to 100% tuition scholarships** (full scholars must avoid failing more than 10% of their SCUAA-enrolled units) and may qualify to stay in the **Athletes' Dormitory** upon coach, dorm dean, and sports head approval. Community game invitations also require a medical permit, parental consent, and Sports Head clearance.

## Cultural and Performing Arts Groups

BulSU's Cultural and Arts Affairs Office recruits students into recognized university performing groups through coach and trainer auditions, including **Hyper-Dynamics**, the university **Band**, **Lahing Kayumanggi**, **Saring Himig**, and **Euphonistas** (plus accredited college cultural clubs).

- **Tuition scholarships and allowance:** Qualified members earn **50% to 100% tuition discounts** (full scholarship plus approved monthly allowance) on their adviser's recommendation.
- **Academic rule:** Full cultural scholars receive an automatic warning upon one failing grade (must pass the next semester) and lose the scholarship after **three (3) failing marks**.
- **Hair color exception:** Cultural performers are exempted from BulSU's natural hair-color rule during official competitions or stage requirements.

## Off-Campus Competition Clearances and Cash Incentives

Whenever any BulSU individual or team leaves campus for official contests, seminars, or guestings, you must file the **Compliance Checklist** at the Office of Student Development, get recommending approval from the OSAS Dean, and secure the **University President's** signature beforehand. Official representation counts as an excused class absence (up to the 30% semester cap).

Under Part IV, Section V (**Students Rewards and Incentives**), winners who represent BulSU at regional or national events earn cash incentives (plus 50% to coaches), alongside Gold Gear Awards recognition:

| Competition Category | Level | 1st Place | 2nd Place | 3rd Place |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Academic, Sports & Cultural** | Regional | P1,000 | P700 | P500 |
| **Academic, Sports & Cultural** | National | P1,500 | P1,200 | P800 |
| **Pageants / Beauty Contests** | Regional | P5,000 | P3,000 | P1,000 |
| **Pageants / Beauty Contests** | National | P10,000 | P8,000 | P5,000 |
