Campus security enforces **No ID, No Entry** at every gate along with daily uniform and grooming rules under Part I, Section D-IV of the Student Manual.

> Altering a photo, sticker, or printed detail on your University ID is a serious offense. Gate guards will confiscate tampered IDs on the spot and escort you straight to your College Dean.

## Campus ID rules and lost ID replacement

Every student must wear a validated BulSU ID while inside campus premises.

### Forgot your ID for the day

Show your **Certificate of Registration (`COR`)** to the gate guard before entering. The guard writes a remark, dates it, and signs over their printed name on the back of the COR to grant temporary entry.

### Replacing a lost ID

1. Have an **Affidavit of Loss** notarized by a notary public. A notarized affidavit lets you enter the campus for up to **3 days** while you process your replacement.
2. Report the loss promptly to both the **Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS)** and the **Office of the Registrar**.
3. Once OSAS and the Registrar authorize replacement, pay the ID fee at the **Cashier** and claim your new card.

If your missing ID turns up before you replace it, report the recovery to OSAS right away so they can cancel the lost-ID record.

## Weekly dress code schedule

| Day / Class | Required Attire | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri | Prescribed daily college or campus uniform | Pair with **black closed shoes**. |
| Friday (optional) | Recognized student organization shirt | Wear the regular daily uniform if not using an org shirt. |
| Wed, Sat, Sun | Free days (civilian attire) | Clothes must not be provocative or offensive to community standards. |
| NSTP and PE sessions | Prescribed NSTP or PE uniform | Wear only during scheduled NSTP or PE periods. |
| Laboratory classes | Prescribed laboratory outfit | Required for safety inside lab rooms. |

Cross-dressing is not permitted during regular school days, with exemptions granted only for special social events such as College Night and Students' Ball.

## Hairstyle, hair color, and jewelry

- Male students with long hair must keep it neatly tied or fixed according to their college or course rules.
- Hair dye is limited to **black, blond, and brown** only, though cultural performers are exempted during competitions and official performances.
- Male students may not wear earrings on campus, and female students are advised against excessive jewelry for personal safety.
