BulSU enforces student discipline progressively, moving from verbal counsel and constructive **Transformative Experience** hours up to suspension, dismissal, or permanent expulsion. Even during serious cases, administrative hearings follow strict filing windows and appeal rights under Part III of the manual.

## Categories of Offenses and Sanctions

Penalties escalate with repeated violations. Committing three different violations triggers an equivalent 2nd Offense penalty from your most serious infraction; four different violations trigger a 3rd Offense penalty.

| Tier | Examples From the Manual | Typical Sanctions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light** | Littering, unauthorized posters/flyers, vandalism, class disruption, unauthorized selling/solicitation | Verbal reprimand -> Written reprimand + 4 hrs Transformational Experience -> 10 hrs + Guidance Intervention (+ restitution/cleaning). 4th offense = 2 weeks suspension. |
| **Less Grave** | Smoking, gambling, alcohol on campus; malicious accusations; fraud/impersonation; disrespecting officials; damaging/entering closed premises | Written reprimand + 4 to 8 hrs Transformational Experience + parent dialogue with Dean -> 10 to 32 hrs + Guidance Intervention (+ restitution). 4th offense = 2 weeks suspension. |
| **Grave** | Theft, lewd indecency on campus -> up to physical/verbal/sexual abuse, cyberbullying, hazing, threats, illegal drugs, carrying weapons | Written reprimand / 1-week to 1-semester suspension -> dismissal -> immediate expulsion (plus police turnover for drugs/weapons or serious injury/death). |
| **Academic Dishonesty** | Cheating in exams; plagiarism in theses/outputs; falsifying/forging academic records or documents | **Cheating:** Zero score (1st), Failing grade in subject + 16 hrs (2nd), 1-month suspension + failing grade (3rd). **Plagiarism:** Fail output -> fail subject + 2-week suspension -> 1-semester suspension. **Forgery:** 1-sem -> 1-yr suspension -> dismissal. |

Note that sanctions like **Withholding of Academic Records/Degree** apply to unpaid debts or pending disciplinary cases, while **Transformative Experience** hours happen during vacant periods so they do not interrupt your classes.

## Formal Hearing Process and Appeal Timelines

Any person may file a sworn written complaint with witness statements and evidence before the college or campus **Student Discipline Committee** (chaired by the College Secretary or Campus equivalent, alongside faculty, Student Governor, PTA, arbiter, and secretary; Student Publications handles press-related cases separately):

1. **Five (5) working days to answer:** If a *prima facie* case exists, the committee sends you the complaint and evidence. You have 5 working days to submit a sworn written answer with supporting documents.
2. **Closed, non-litigious hearing:** Held within 5 working days of your answer to clarify facts. You may bring legal counsel at your own expense and submit questions through the committee. Failing to appear after proper notice lets the hearing proceed without you.
3. **Committee recommendation -> Board of Student Discipline:** A majority vote goes to the Board of Student Discipline within 5 working days; the Board reviews and issues its decision within 5 working days.
4. **Ten (10) working days for Motion for Reconsideration:** Either party has 10 working days from receipt of the decision to file a Motion for Reconsideration with the Board.
5. **Final appeal for Dismissal or Expulsion:** If your motion is denied and the sanction is **dismissal** (at least 1 year exclusion, signed by the President) or **expulsion** (permanent severance noted on your TOR), you have **10 working days** to appeal to the **University President or Board of Regents**.

**Summary Proceedings** skip formal hearings when a respondent admits guilt in writing, is caught in the act by authorities, faces strong evidence, or is a habitual offender (punished 3+ times prior).

## Can a Suspension Record Be Expunged Later?

Suspensions of at least one semester go onto your permanent record. After **three (3) years** from the finding date, you or a former student may petition the **University President** (or designee) to expunge the record based on violation severity, overall record, and proof of good behavior since. Once granted, the suspension notation is removed from your Registrar academic file.

To report unfair treatment against **you** by any member of the campus community, read [How to File a Student Complaint](/articles/how-to-file-a-student-complaint).
