Yes, class attendance at BulSU is strictly tracked, and showing up late or skipping early carries exact minute thresholds. Only students listed on the official Management Information System (MIS) Office enrollment list may attend class (sit-in students require permission from the academic unit head).

## Late and Absent Cutoffs

- **15 minutes late = Tardy:** Arriving 15 minutes after the scheduled start marks you tardy.
- **20 minutes late = Absent:** Arriving 20 minutes after class starts marks you absent. **Your professor cannot bar you from entering the lesson** if you still want to attend and listen.

## How Long Students Must Wait for a Late Instructor

Unless your professor sent prior notice, you may leave the assigned classroom if the teacher has not arrived after:

| Class Duration | Required Wait Time |
| --- | --- |
| **1-hour class** | 15 minutes |
| **2-hour class** | 30 minutes |
| **3-hour class** | 45 minutes |

Walking out **before** the required wait time marks the class absent.

## Excused Absences (Max 30% Cap)

Absences are excused only for three reasons:

1. **Official representation** in curricular, co-curricular, or extra-curricular activities (for example, official varsity meets, press conferences, or cultural competitions).
2. **Sickness certified** by an attending physician or the BulSU University Physician.
3. **Force majeure** (natural disasters or sudden unavoidable emergencies).

Two catches apply even when your excuse is approved: excused absences **never exempt you from submitting regular course requirements**, and total excused absences cannot exceed **30% of required semester attendance**.

## Unexcused Absences Trigger Automatic FDA / UD (20% Cap)

If you accumulate **more than 20% unexcused absences** in a subject **before midterm examinations**, you are **dropped automatically** from that class. Your instructor marks **`FDA` (Failure Due to Absences)** or **`UD` (Unofficially Dropped)** on your grade sheet.

That automatic `FDA` or `UD` mark hurts twice under [What Happens When You Fail a Subject?](/articles/what-happens-when-you-fail-a-subject) and carries zero refund under [How to Add, Change, or Drop Subjects](/articles/how-to-add-change-or-drop-subjects). If a prolonged illness forces you to miss class, notify the BulSU Medical Clinic and look into [How to File a Leave of Absence](/articles/how-to-file-a-leave-of-absence).
