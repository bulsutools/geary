Off-campus trips run by BulSU colleges follow Commission on Higher Education rules: **`CMO 63, s. 2017`** for local educational field trips and **`CMO 26, s. 2015`** for international educational tours. Both orders protect students against mandatory fees, punitive grading, and unsafe transit.

## Local educational trips (CHED CMO 63, s. 2017)

Participation in off-campus field trips is **voluntary**. Colleges must give students who cannot join **non-punitive parallel activities** with equivalent learning outcomes, and may never replace a missed trip with a major examination or deduct grades for opting out.

- **Advance notice & consultation:** Fees and destinations require prior student consultation, announced **1 to 2 months** before departure alongside a mandatory general orientation.
- **Cost rule:** Organizers may charge **actual travel and activity costs only** without extra administrative markups.
- **Student clearance items:** Official enrollment, subscribed or notarized parent or guardian consent, medical clearance from a university or government physician, valid insurance, and a field learning journal.
- **Safety and supervision:** One Person-in-Charge (`PIC`) must supervise every **`35 to 50`** students. Vehicles must carry verified `LTFRB` franchises, `DOT` accreditation, and roadworthiness checks; students are strictly barred from riding on vehicle roofs or boarding platforms.

## International educational trips (CHED CMO 26, s. 2015)

Only academic programs holding at least **Level II accreditation** (or Autonomous, Deregulated, Center of Excellence/Development, or `ISA` status) may organize international study tours, which must be notarized and filed with `CHEDRO` at least **30 working days** before departure.

- Participation remains **voluntary**, with local academic alternatives available for meritorious cases.
- Participants must be at least **`18` years old** (minors require `DSWD` travel clearance), hold a valid passport, keep good academic standing, and pass medical screening.
- Required forms include a notarized statement of intent, signed consent from a parent, guardian, or spouse, and endorsement from the University President.

Universities are **strictly prohibited from demanding non-liability waivers** from parents or students for either local or international educational activities.
