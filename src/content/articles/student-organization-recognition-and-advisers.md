The **Office of Student Organizations and Activities (`OSOA`)** regulates student group recognition, annual renewal, faculty advisers, and the campus fraternity cap. Existing organizations renew their status every **August to September**, leading into the *Balangayan* awarding ceremony in September.

## Member minimums and application timeline

- New organizations need at least **15 student members** to apply for recognition.
- Once recognized, an organization must maintain at least **8 regular members in good standing** to keep active status.
- `OSOA` acts on applications within **2 weeks** of submission.
- If recognition is denied, the group may appeal to the **Dean of OSAS** within **10 days**, and OSAS decides within **10 working days**.

New recognition packets require seven documents: constitution and by-laws, officer and member profiles, annual calendar of activities, adviser invitation letter, adviser acceptance letter, and voluntary membership forms (groups with 50 or more members submit a hardcopy member list instead of individual forms). Annual renewals add an **accomplishment report** and an **audited financial report**.

## Adviser selection and term limits

Organizations pick **1 to 3 advisers** serving a renewable **1-year term**. When appointing two advisers, one is designated senior adviser; three advisers form a Board of Advisers with an elected Chair.

Advisers must be full-time regular faculty or staff. Part-time faculty with at least **two consecutive semesters** of service may advise only if the college or satellite campus has no available regular faculty. An ineffective adviser can be removed through `OSOA` mediation or by vote under the organization's constitution.

## Fraternities and non-recognized groups

BulSU caps recognized fraternities at **three (3) university-wide**. Any violation of the **Anti-Hazing Law (`RA 8094`)** triggers automatic disqualification alongside university administrative sanctions and legal action.

When a sworn complaint accuses a recognized group of violations, `OSOA` forwards the case to the **Dean of OSAS** and **Board of Student Discipline (`BSD`)** within **5 working days**. Non-recognized groups cannot use BulSU's name, duplicate programs already assigned to recognized organizations, or stage campus activities without express permission from the Dean of OSAS.
