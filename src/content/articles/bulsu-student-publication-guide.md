Campus journalism at BulSU runs under the **Office of Student Publications and Information (OSPI)** in line with the **Campus Journalism Act of 1991 (Republic Act 7079)**. Whether you want to write for BulSU's flagship paper or join a college publication, clear eligibility rules and selection exams apply.

## The Pacesetter and College Publications

- **University-wide flagship:** ***The Pacesetter*** is BulSU's official university student publication, circulated across the main campus and satellite campuses and funded by the student school paper fee held in a special trust fund.
- **College and campus papers:** OSPI also recognizes local publications across individual colleges and external campuses, hosting press workshops and conferences to train student writers and editors.

OSPI upholds editorial independence and handles formal complaints tied to campus publications separately from the regular college Student Discipline Committees.

## How to Qualify for the Editorial Board

To apply for any editorial board position (at *The Pacesetter* or a college publication), you must meet four baseline requirements:

1. Enrolled in **at least 15 academic units** certified by your unit head or the University Registrar.
2. Completed **at least one (1) year of residence** at BulSU.
3. Have **no failing (`5.00`), incomplete (`Inc.`), or conditional (`4.00`) mark** in any subject.
4. Have **zero record of disciplinary action**.

## Selection Process, Term Limits, and Removal Rules

Editorial board applicants take written examinations and interviews run by an ad hoc selection committee made up of the Head of Student Publications and Information, one faculty member, one jointly accepted media practitioner, and two past editors chosen by the outgoing editorial board. Qualifiers are endorsed to the University President for official designation.

- **One-year term:** Members serve a one-year term ending at the close of the academic year, with the next board selected **at least 30 days before** the academic year ends.
- **Removal grounds:** Board members and staff may be removed after due process for incompetence, unauthorized affiliation with outside groups, misuse of publication funds or property, defamatory write-ups, mocking athletes or physical disabilities, invading personal privacy, immorality, or breaking university rules.

## Campus Journalism Training and Contests

BulSU campus journalists regularly train and compete in local, regional, and national press events recognized in the manual, including *Pandayan Plaridel*, *Gawad Galing Plaridel*, the Regional Tertiary Press Congress, the Regional Higher Education Press Conference (RHEPC), the Annual National CONFAB, the Luzon-wide Higher Education Press Conference, and Best Literary Concept. Top campus writers can also earn the **Journalist of the Year** recognition at the annual Gold Gear Awards.
