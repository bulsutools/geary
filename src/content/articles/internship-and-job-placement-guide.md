Domestic internships follow `CHED CMO 09, s. 2013` under the **Career Development and Student Internship (`CarDSI`)** office and college **SIP Coordinators**, while international internships run through the **Director for International Affairs** under `CMO 22, s. 2013`. Graduating seniors and job seekers can also use the **Placement and Public Employment Service Office (`PPESO`)**.

## Student Internship Program (SIP) placement steps

Completing the SIP placement sequence is mandatory before reporting to a training site; **students who fail to complete this placement process are automatically de-registered from their internship course**.

1. Attend the college internship orientation conducted by your SIP Coordinator.
2. Fill out the **Student-Intern Information Sheet**.
3. Choose **three Host Training Establishments (`HTE`s)** from the approved partner list. If you prefer a company outside the list, notify your SIP Coordinator ahead of time so the office can process accreditation.
4. Contact your selected establishments and schedule placement interviews.
5. Prepare and submit the final placement documents signed by **you, the HTE representative, your SIP Coordinator, and your parent or guardian**.

## Expected practices during internship

Before starting work hours, contact your site supervisor several days in advance and notify your SIP Coordinator as soon as your training hours begin. Complete the company's own orientation, maintain a regular analytical field learning journal, attend required campus internship seminars, coordinate faculty site visits, and wrap up your exit clearance smoothly with the host company. You will also need an OJT medical clearance from the [BulSU Clinic](/articles/clinic-and-student-insurance) (Chest X-Ray with official radiologist reading).

## PPESO employment programs

`PPESO` connects students and alumni with local employers through annual **Jobs Fairs** and partner government programs:

- **SPES (Special Program for Employment of Students):** Summer and Christmas break work placements for deserving students.
- **TULAY:** Training and employment support tailored for persons with disabilities (`PWDs`).
- **WHIP:** Work placement coordination aligned with `RA 6685` infrastructure projects.

`PPESO` handles domestic employment matching only and **does not conduct overseas job placement**. Students seeking work abroad are referred directly to `POEA` and licensed overseas recruitment agencies.
