Student writers, cultural performers, and varsity competitors represent BulSU through recognized publications, performing arts ensembles, and inter-school tournaments governed by `RA 7079` and OSAS award policies.

## Student publications and The Pacesetter (RA 7079)

BulSU's official student publication is ***The Pacesetter***, alongside recognized college and satellite campus papers. Editorial board members serve a **1-year term** selected at least **30 days** before the academic year closes, and must meet four standing checks:

- Enrolled in at least **`15` academic units**.
- At least **`1 year` of continuous residency** at BulSU.
- No failing (`5.00`), `Inc.`, or `4.00` marks in current or evaluated records.
- Clear disciplinary record (eight specified offenses in the manual trigger board removal).

## Cultural and performing arts groups

Students who qualify through auditions for recognized university performing arts ensembles receive a **full tuition scholarship** plus an administration-approved monthly allowance ([Institutional scholarships](/articles/institutional-scholarships)).

Academic standing is reviewed every semester: **one failing grade** results in a warning requiring you to pass the next semester, while **three failing grades trigger automatic removal** from the cultural scholarship.

## Official cash incentives for competition winners

Under the Student Manual incentives schedule (Part III, Cultural & Arts / Student Incentives), students who win in authorized inter-school events qualify for cash awards, with coaches of academic, sports, and cultural teams receiving **50%** of the student award rate:

| Competition Category | Level & Placement | Official Cash Incentive |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Academic, Sports & Cultural | Regional (`1st / 2nd / 3rd`) | `P1,000` / `P700` / `P500` |
| Academic, Sports & Cultural | National (`1st / 2nd / 3rd`) | `P1,500` / `P1,200` / `P800` |
| Beauty Contests | Regional (`1st / 2nd / 3rd`) | `P5,000` / `P3,000` / `P1,000` |
| Beauty Contests | National | Paid per current Awards committee schedule. |
