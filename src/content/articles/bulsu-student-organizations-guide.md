Student life at BulSU runs largely through groups accredited by the **Office of Student Organizations and Activities (OSOA)** and its student arm, the **BulSU-OSO Executive Board**. From college academic societies to cultural, political, and multi-faith clubs, here is how organizations are recognized and run.

## Can Freshmen Join Multiple Organizations?

**Yes.** Under Part IV, Section III.A, membership in recognized organizations (including local chapters of extramural groups) is **voluntary and open to all students** of the university. Nothing bars freshmen from signing up or students from joining more than one club, though organizations run on their own constitutions and voting membership rules.

Recognized groups fall into seven classifications:
- **College-based / Satellite campus-based organizations** tied to specific degree programs
- **Socio-Civic, Environmental, Lifestyle, Political, and Multi-Faith groups** (OSOA stays faith-neutral and supports inter-faith activities)
- **Fraternities:** BulSU caps university-wide recognition at a **maximum of three (3) fraternities** at a time. Any fraternity caught breaking university rules or Republic Act 8049 (**Anti-Hazing Law**) faces automatic revocation of recognition plus legal action.

Only recognized organizations may carry the BulSU name, reserve campus facilities, collect member dues, hold events, or sell reasonably priced merchandise like org shirts (permitted optionally on Fridays).

## Starting or Renewing a Student Organization

Every recognized org must maintain at least **8 regular enrolled members in good standing** and have a full-time BulSU faculty or non-teaching employee serving as **adviser** (up to 3 advisers max, for a 1-year renewable term; part-time faculty with 2 consecutive semesters at BulSU qualify only if a regular adviser is unavailable).

- **New organizations:** Need at least **15 founding members** submitting the application form, constitution and by-laws, interim officer/member signatures, annual activity plan, adviser invitation and signed acceptance letter, and signed voluntary membership forms (lists suffice for 50+ members). OSOA notifies you within **2 weeks**.
- **Annual renewal (August and September):** Existing orgs renew each August-September with updated officer rosters, audited financial statements (with official receipts/invoices and thermal receipt photocopies), and an accomplishment report with photos and approved letters. Recognized orgs receive certificates every September during **Balangayan sa Unibersidad (Organizations' Week)**.

If OSOA denies recognition, you have **10 days** to appeal in writing to the **Dean of OSAS**, who decides within **10 working days**.

## Running Events, Room Bookings, Fundraising, and Posters

- **Reserving campus rooms:** Submit requests **at least one (1) week ahead** after checking availability with the facility unit head. Scheduled academic classes always get first priority, and one org cannot reserve a room on another group's behalf.
- **On-campus vs. off-campus events:** All events require the president's and adviser's signatures plus the **Compliance Checklist**. On-campus events go to the **VPAA** (or Campus Administrator on satellite campuses), while off-campus events escalate through OSOA, OSD, OSAS Dean, and VPAA to the **University President**. All off-campus events and external speakers require adherence to safety checks and no illegal/propaganda recruitment.
- **Fundraising:** Each org is limited to **one (1) major fundraising activity per academic year**, approved by the President (plus a **DSWD permit** if collecting off campus), followed by a financial report to OSOA within **2 weeks**.
- **Campus posters and flyers:** Submit a signed **letter of intent** plus the original poster to **OSOA** (and get College Dean permission for college buildings). Use only **scotch tape, masking tape, or push pins** on designated bulletin boards for a maximum of **2 weeks**, then take everything down the day after expiration.
